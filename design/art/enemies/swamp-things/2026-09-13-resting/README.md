# Swamp Things resting concepts

Five separate full-body concepts: Crawler, Lurker, Spitter, Grasper and Old Thing. Roles follow the GDD Swamp Things roster; anatomical designs are proposed art for review. No game models are replaced.

Crawler and Spitter are cutouts. Lurker, Grasper and Old Thing retain dark studio backgrounds despite the transparency request. A Lurker background-removal attempt also retained the backdrop and was not selected.
