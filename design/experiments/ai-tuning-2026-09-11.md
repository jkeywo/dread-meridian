# AI weight tuning, 11 September 2026

Headless co-evolution of the utility-AI weights for the smuggler sandbox. All
numbers are sandbox tuning evidence, not approved balance (AGENTS.md). Method:
`docs/ai-tuning.md`; harness: `tools/tune_ai.py`. Every run is deterministic per
seed (`-deterministic -fps=60`, spawn jitter from the RunGeneration stream).

Fitness (seed averages): enemy = damage to investigators + 150 x investigator
downs + 2000 if defeat - 0.5 x damage to enemies - 0.2 x tick; bot = damage to
enemies + 300 x enemy kills + 3000 if victory - damage to investigators - 200 x
investigator downs - 0.5 x tick. Higher is better for the side named.

## Campaign 1 (aborted)

Seeds 1927-1930, 8 candidates, 6 generations. Stopped after the first generation:
three of seven mutants reproduced the incumbent's runs exactly because their knobs
(enemy Engage weight, Signature weight, an ability Base away from its boundary)
are behaviourally inert under rank ordering with one option per channel. The knob
table was rewritten around latch thresholds, runtime and cooldown caps, ranges,
worth multipliers and ping compliance (113 knobs across nine profiles, each
verified to apply through `-DMAIWeights` without warnings).

## Hold-out, thirty untouched seeds (1951-1980)

| Setup | Wins / 30 | Bot fitness | Enemy fitness |
|---|---|---|---|
| defaults vs defaults | 2 | 3585 +- 354 | 2044 +- 136 |
| campaign-3 enemies vs default bots | 1 | 3734 +- 270 | 1984 +- 104 |
| default enemies vs campaign-3 bots | 5 | 4820 +- 338 | 1723 +- 161 |
| campaign-3 enemies vs campaign-3 bots | 7 | 5258 +- 429 | 1445 +- 211 |

The bot tuning generalises: against default enemies bot fitness rises by about a
third and wins go from 2 to 5 of 30 (7 of 30 with both tuned). The enemy tuning
does not: against either bot set the campaign-3 enemies are within one standard
error of the defaults with a slightly negative point estimate. Their training
gains were seed-fitting that four validation seeds did not catch.

## Campaign 4 (robust enemy-only pass)

Enemy knobs reset to defaults, tuned against the campaign-3 bots. Twelve training
seeds (2001-2012), eight validation seeds (2021-2028), 5 % minimum gain, mutation
step 0.3 of each knob's range, 10 candidates, stale after 3.

Training 2177.2 to 2432.6 in six generations (two accepted, both confirmed on the
validation seeds: 1898 to 1953 to 2127). Seven knobs moved: Bomber
TargetCommitment 52.6, Bruiser LeashRange 1492, Gunman KeepDistance
MaxRuntimeTicks 27 and SightRange 612, Lookout AllyRadius 1011, EliteWorth 0.97
and TargetCommitment 13.8.

Hold-out, the same thirty seeds:

| Setup | Wins / 30 | Bot fitness | Enemy fitness |
|---|---|---|---|
| campaign-4 enemies vs default bots | 1 | 3389 +- 318 | 2112 +- 113 |
| campaign-4 enemies vs campaign-3 bots | 4 | 4441 +- 385 | 1792 +- 183 |

Against both bot sets the campaign-4 enemies are better than the defaults in
point estimate (2044 to 2112, 1723 to 1792; bot wins 5 to 4) but inside one
standard error. That is the honest state of enemy tuning within this budget: a
small, consistent, unproven gain, versus a clear gain for the bots.

## Decision and baked result

Baked: the campaign-3 investigator knobs and the campaign-4 enemy knobs
(`Saved/AITuning/final/best.json`, 44 values). The campaign-3 enemy knobs were
discarded because they did not generalise. Enemy strength in this sandbox is
dominated by fixed rules (threat, leash, telegraphs, signature cooldowns), which
the tunable knobs move only at the margins; further enemy gains need new
behaviours, not more weight search.
